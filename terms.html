Bank connections through Plaid

You can link bank accounts through Plaid Inc. You sign in to your bank inside Plaid's own window, so the app never sees your bank username or password.

By connecting a bank, you allow Plaid to collect your account names, balances and posted transactions from that bank and pass them to the app. Plaid's handling of your information is covered by its own End User Privacy Policy.

You can disconnect a bank at any time under Settings, which also ends Plaid's access to it. Transactions already imported stay in the app until you delete them.
